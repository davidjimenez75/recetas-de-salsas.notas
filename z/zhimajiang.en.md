# Chinese sesame sauce (zhimajiang)

![Chinese sesame sauce (zhimajiang)](zhimajiang.jpg)

*Photo: [Fumikas Sagisavas](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sesame_paste.jpg), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

Toasted sesame paste thinned with soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar and water: the dipping sauce for Beijing hot pot, cold noodles and smashed cucumbers. The photo shows sesame paste.

🥄 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 10 minutes · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Salsa de sésamo china (zhimajiang)](zhimajiang.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons toasted sesame paste (zhimajiang, or toasted tahini)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Chinkiang black vinegar (or rice vinegar)
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 garlic clove, grated (optional)
- 4 to 6 tablespoons warm water
- Chopped scallion and cilantro

## Directions

1. **Loosen the paste.** Put the sesame paste in a bowl and add the water a spoonful at a time, whisking. It seizes at first; keep going until creamy and pourable.
2. **Season.** Add the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, sesame oil and the garlic, if using.
3. **Whisk.** Whisk until smooth and as thick as heavy cream.
4. **Taste.** Adjust the salt with more soy sauce or the tang with more vinegar.
5. **Serve.** Topped with scallion and cilantro, in small bowls for hot pot or over cold noodles.

## Tips

- **Toasted tahini:** regular tahini is paler and milder; toast it in a dry pan or add more sesame oil.
- **No heat:** chile oil is classic but optional; serve it on the side.
- **With noodles:** toss with cold noodles, shredded cucumber and thin egg strips.
