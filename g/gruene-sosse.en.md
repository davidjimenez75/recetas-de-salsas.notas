# Frankfurt green sauce (Grüne Soße)

![Frankfurt green sauce (Grüne Soße)](gruene-sosse.jpg)

*Photo: [Gerda Arendt](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gr%C3%BCne_So%C3%9Fe,_R%C3%BCdesheim.jpg), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

Seven fresh herbs chopped with sour cream, yogurt and hard-boiled egg: the cold sauce from Frankfurt, served with hard-boiled eggs and boiled potatoes, as in the photo.

🥄 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 20 minutes + 1 hour resting · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Grüne Soße (salsa verde de Fráncfort)](gruene-sosse.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 7 oz fresh herbs in total: parsley, chives, watercress, sorrel, borage, chervil and salad burnet
- ¾ cup sour cream (or crème fraîche)
- ¾ cup plain yogurt
- 2 hard-boiled eggs (plus 4 to 8 more for serving)
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice or vinegar
- Salt, pepper and a pinch of sugar

## Directions

1. **Herbs.** Wash them, dry them well and remove the tough stems.
2. **Chop.** Chop them very finely by hand, or pulse them in a food processor without making a purée.
3. **Base.** Mash the yolks of the 2 eggs with the oil and mustard, then stir in the sour cream, yogurt and lemon.
4. **Mix.** Add the herbs and chopped egg whites, season and add the sugar.
5. **Rest.** Chill for 1 hour. Serve with halved hard-boiled eggs and boiled potatoes.

## Tips

- **Missing herbs:** if you can't find borage or burnet, use more parsley, chives and watercress, plus a little dill.
- **Maundy Thursday** is the traditional day to eat it in Hesse.
- **With meat:** it also goes with boiled beef and schnitzel.
