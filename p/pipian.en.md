# Green pumpkin seed sauce

![Green pumpkin seed sauce](pipian.jpg)

*Photo: [Joselu Blanco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Enchiladas_de_carnitas_de_pato._Salsa_de_pipi%C3%A1n_verde._(Puebla,_Puebla).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

Toasted pumpkin seeds with tomatillo, poblano chile, lettuce and cilantro: a creamy, mild green mole.

🥄 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 40 minutes · Difficulty: medium · Heat: mild

*En español:* [Pipián verde](pipian.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup hulled pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
- 5 tomatillos
- ½ onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 poblano chile, roasted, peeled and seeded
- 2 romaine lettuce leaves
- 1 handful cilantro
- 2 cups chicken stock (or vegetable stock)
- 2 tablespoons oil
- Salt

## Directions

1. **Toast.** Toast the pepitas in a dry pan over medium-low heat, stirring, until they start to pop. Don't let them burn or they turn bitter.
2. **Tomatillos.** Husk them and simmer in water for 5 minutes, until they change color.
3. **Blend.** Blend the pepitas with the tomatillos, onion, garlic, poblano, lettuce, cilantro and some stock until smooth.
4. **Simmer.** Fry the sauce in the oil over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring. Add the remaining stock and simmer gently 15 minutes.
5. **Adjust.** Salt at the end. Don't let it boil hard or the sauce will split.

## Tips

- **Serve with** chicken, pork, enchiladas or roasted vegetables.
- **Vegan:** use vegetable stock.
- **Poblano** is a mild chile; remove all seeds and veins.
