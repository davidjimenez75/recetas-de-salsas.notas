# Melitzanosalata

![Melitzanosalata](melitzanosalata.jpg)

*Photo: [Badseed](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eggplant_salad.JPG), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

Eggplant roasted until smoky, mashed with garlic, olive oil, lemon and parsley: the Greek eggplant dip for pita bread.

🥄 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 50 minutes · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Melitzanosalata](melitzanosalata.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggplants (about 1¾ lb)
- 1 garlic clove
- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice or red wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- ½ small red onion, finely chopped (optional)
- Salt

## Directions

1. **Roast.** Prick the eggplants and roast them whole at 425 °F for 40 minutes, or over a gas flame, turning, until the skin is charred and the flesh very soft.
2. **Drain.** Split them, scoop out the flesh and let it drain in a colander for 10 minutes to remove the bitter liquid.
3. **Mash.** Chop it with a knife or mash with a fork; it should keep some texture.
4. **Season.** Add the grated garlic, salt and lemon, then the oil a little at a time, stirring.
5. **Finish.** Stir in the parsley and the onion, if using. Serve cold with pita bread.

## Tips

- **Smoke is the key:** charring it over a flame or on the grill gives the classic flavor.
- **No blender:** make it by hand; a blender turns it gluey.
- **Variation:** some families add chopped walnuts or crumbled feta (then it is no longer vegan).
