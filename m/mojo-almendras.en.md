# Canarian almond mojo

![Canarian almond mojo](mojo-almendras.jpg)

*Photo: [Tamorlan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Almendra_marcona.jpg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

Toasted almonds, garlic, bread, cumin, olive oil and vinegar pounded in a mortar: a Canarian mojo from La Palma, vegan, with no alcohol and no peppers. The photo shows the almonds, not the mojo.

🥄 Serves **6** · ⏱️ 15 minutes · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Mojo de almendras](mojo-almendras.es.md)

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup toasted almonds
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 slice stale bread
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coarse salt
- A few saffron threads (optional)
- Scant ½ cup olive oil
- 2–3 tablespoons wine vinegar
- A little water

## Directions

1. **Bread.** Soak the bread in the vinegar.
2. **Pound.** In a mortar, crush the cumin with the salt, garlic and saffron into a paste.
3. **Almonds.** Add the almonds and the squeezed bread and pound into a coarse paste.
4. **Emulsify.** Stream in the oil while stirring, then thin with a little water and the leftover vinegar until thick.

## Tips

- **Serve with** Canarian wrinkled potatoes, fish, rabbit or roasted vegetables.
- **A blender** makes it smoother; a mortar, more rustic.
- **Keeps** 3–4 days in the fridge in a sealed jar.
