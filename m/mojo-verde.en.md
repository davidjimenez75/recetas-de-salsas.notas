# Canarian green cilantro sauce

![Canarian green cilantro sauce](mojo-verde.jpg)

*Photo: [Abhay Kumar from San Francisco, CA, USA](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mojo_verde_y_papas_arrug%C3%A1s.jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

The Canarian mojo of cilantro (or parsley), garlic, cumin, olive oil and vinegar, a classic with fish.

🥄 Serves **6** · ⏱️ 10 minutes · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Mojo verde](mojo-verde.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch cilantro (or parsley, or half and half)
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 small green bell pepper (optional)
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coarse salt
- Scant ½ cup olive oil
- 2–3 tablespoons wine vinegar

## Directions

1. **Prep.** Wash and dry the cilantro well and remove the thickest stems.
2. **Pound.** In a mortar, crush the cumin with the salt and garlic.
3. **Herbs.** Add the chopped cilantro and pepper and pound into a paste.
4. **Emulsify.** Stream in the oil while stirring, then the vinegar. Taste and adjust.

## Tips

- **Serve with** grilled or poached fish and Canarian wrinkled potatoes.
- **Blender version:** everything in the cup, adding the oil last.
- **Best the same day:** cilantro loses its color over time.
